**A. Creation of Trust.** Notwithstanding any other provision of this Will, any property that would otherwise pass outright to {SN_BENEFICIARY_NAME} (referred to in this Article as the “Beneficiary”) under this Will, whether as a specific bequest, as a share of the residue of my estate, as a remainder beneficiary of any trust created under this Will, or otherwise, shall not be distributed to the Beneficiary. Such property shall instead be distributed to the Trustee named in this Article, to be held, administered, and distributed as a separate trust under the terms of this Article. This trust shall be known as the “{SN_BENEFICIARY_NAME} Supplemental Needs Trust.” If the Beneficiary does not survive me, this Article shall have no effect.

**B. Purpose and Intent.** The Beneficiary has a disability, and I wish to provide for the Beneficiary’s care and quality of life without jeopardizing the Beneficiary’s eligibility for public assistance. I intend this trust to be a discretionary, third-party supplemental needs trust. The purpose of this trust is to supplement, and not to supplant, impair, or diminish, any benefits or assistance of any federal, state, county, city, or other governmental or private program for which the Beneficiary is or may become eligible, including without limitation Supplemental Security Income, Medicaid (TennCare), and housing assistance. This trust is funded solely with my property, and no part of this trust consists of property of the Beneficiary. I do not intend that the income or principal of this trust be considered a resource or income available to the Beneficiary for purposes of determining eligibility for any such program. This Article shall be construed, and this trust administered, in a manner consistent with that intent.

**C. Trustee.** I appoint my {TRUSTEE_RELATION}, {TRUSTEE_NAME}, as Trustee of this trust.

[[IF HAS_ALT_TRUSTEE]]If {TRUSTEE_NAME} is unable or unwilling to serve or to continue serving, I appoint my {ALT_TRUSTEE_RELATION}, {ALT_TRUSTEE_NAME}, as successor Trustee.[[END IF]]

If no Trustee named in this Article is able and willing to serve, the last serving Trustee may designate a successor Trustee by written instrument, and if no successor is so designated, a successor Trustee shall be appointed by a court of competent jurisdiction. Any individual or corporate fiduciary may serve as successor Trustee, but in no event shall the Beneficiary serve as Trustee. No Trustee shall be required to post bond. The Trustee shall be entitled to reasonable compensation for services and to reimbursement of expenses properly incurred.

**D. Discretionary Distributions.** The Trustee may distribute to or for the benefit of the Beneficiary so much of the net income and principal of this trust as the Trustee, in the Trustee’s sole and absolute discretion, determines advisable to provide for the Beneficiary’s supplemental needs, meaning needs that are not otherwise provided for by any governmental or private program. Supplemental needs may include, by way of illustration and not limitation: medical, dental, vision, and therapeutic care and equipment not otherwise covered; education, vocational training, and tutoring; recreation, travel, and the expenses of a companion; transportation, including the purchase and maintenance of a vehicle; computers, telephones, and other electronic equipment; personal care attendants and case management; advocacy, legal, guardianship, and conservatorship expenses; and other goods and services that enhance the Beneficiary’s dignity, independence, and quality of life. Any net income not distributed shall be added to principal.

Whenever practicable, the Trustee shall make distributions directly to the providers of goods and services rather than to the Beneficiary in cash.

**E. No Right to Compel Distributions.** The Beneficiary shall have no right or power to compel any distribution from this trust or to direct the Trustee to make any distribution. This trust is not a support trust, and no interest of the Beneficiary in this trust shall be considered available to the Beneficiary. The Trustee’s exercise of discretion under this Article shall be conclusive on all persons and shall not be subject to review by any court except for a failure to act in good faith.

**F. Distributions Affecting Public Benefits.** Before making any distribution, the Trustee shall consider its effect on the Beneficiary’s eligibility for public benefits. Notwithstanding the supplemental purpose of this trust, the Trustee may make a distribution for food, shelter, or any other purpose, even if the distribution will reduce or temporarily suspend a public benefit, if the Trustee determines in the Trustee’s sole discretion that the benefit to the Beneficiary from the distribution outweighs the resulting reduction or loss. However, the Trustee shall not make any distribution that would cause the Beneficiary to lose eligibility for Medicaid (TennCare) or other medical assistance unless the Trustee determines that the Beneficiary’s medical needs will be adequately met from other sources. The Trustee shall not be liable to the Beneficiary or to any other person for any reduction or loss of benefits resulting from a distribution made in good faith under this Section.

**G. Governmental Requests; No Reimbursement.** No part of the income or principal of this trust shall be used to reimburse any governmental entity for benefits provided to the Beneficiary. If any agency or department administering TennCare or any other public benefits program requests that the Trustee release trust income or principal to pay for equipment, medication, services, or other items that the program is authorized to provide, the Trustee shall deny the request. Because this trust is funded solely with property of persons other than the Beneficiary, no state or other governmental entity shall have any claim against this trust for reimbursement of benefits provided to the Beneficiary, whether during the Beneficiary’s lifetime or after the Beneficiary’s death.

**H. ABLE Account.** The Trustee may, in the Trustee’s sole discretion, contribute trust income or principal to an account established for the Beneficiary under a qualified ABLE program described in Section 529A of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986, as amended, including the ABLE TN program, subject to applicable contribution limits. In deciding whether to make such a contribution, the Trustee shall consider that amounts remaining in an ABLE account at the Beneficiary’s death may be subject to a claim for reimbursement of Medicaid benefits, while property remaining in this trust is not.

**I. Additions to the Trust.** Any person other than the Beneficiary may add property to this trust, by will, beneficiary designation, or otherwise, with the consent of the Trustee. The Trustee shall not accept any property that belongs to the Beneficiary or to which the Beneficiary has a legal right, including property the Beneficiary receives by gift, inheritance, settlement, judgment, or support obligation. If such property is offered, the Trustee shall not commingle it with this trust, but may assist in establishing a separate trust or other lawful arrangement for that property, including a trust described in 42 U.S.C. § 1396p(d)(4)(A) or (C).

**J. Spendthrift Provision.** The interest of the Beneficiary and of each remainder beneficiary in this trust shall be held subject to a spendthrift trust to the fullest extent permitted by Tennessee law. No interest in the income or principal of this trust shall be voluntarily or involuntarily transferred, assigned, anticipated, pledged, or encumbered, nor shall any such interest be subject to the claims of any creditor of a beneficiary, including any claim for reimbursement by a governmental entity, or to attachment, garnishment, execution, or other legal process, before actual receipt by the beneficiary.

**K. Power to Amend to Preserve Eligibility.** If the Trustee determines that any provision of this Article, or any change in law or in the rules of any public benefits program, may cause trust property to be considered available to the Beneficiary or may otherwise jeopardize the Beneficiary’s eligibility for public benefits, the Trustee, other than the Beneficiary, may amend the administrative and technical provisions of this Article, without court approval, to the extent necessary to preserve the Beneficiary’s eligibility and to carry out my intent. The Trustee may also, to the extent permitted by Tennessee law, distribute trust property to the trustee of another trust for the Beneficiary’s benefit that is intended to qualify as a third-party supplemental needs trust and that provides for the same remainder beneficiaries as this trust. No amendment or distribution under this Section shall grant the Beneficiary any power over this trust, make trust property available for the Beneficiary’s support, or change the remainder beneficiaries.

**L. Early Termination.** If the Trustee determines that continued administration of this trust is uneconomical, or that the existence of this trust jeopardizes the Beneficiary’s eligibility for needed public benefits in a manner that cannot be cured under Section K, the Trustee may terminate this trust. Upon termination, the Trustee shall distribute the remaining trust property to the trustee of another third-party supplemental needs trust for the Beneficiary’s benefit, which may include a third-party sub-account of a pooled trust maintained by a nonprofit association, or, if the Trustee determines that no such arrangement is reasonably available, to the persons described in Section M as though the Beneficiary had died on the date of termination. In no event shall trust property be distributed outright to the Beneficiary upon termination. This Section shall govern in lieu of any statutory provision that would otherwise authorize or require distribution of a terminated trust to the Beneficiary.

**M. Distribution upon Death of Beneficiary.** Upon the death of the Beneficiary, the Trustee may pay, to the extent not otherwise provided for, the reasonable expenses of the Beneficiary’s last illness, funeral, and burial, and any taxes attributable to this trust that are due by reason of the Beneficiary’s death. Subject to Section N, the Trustee shall distribute the remaining trust property to the Beneficiary’s then living issue, per stirpes, or, if none, [[IF HAS_CONTINGENT_REMAINDER]]to my {REMAINDER_RELATION}, {REMAINDER_NAME}, or, if {REMAINDER_NAME} is not then living, to the then living issue of {REMAINDER_NAME}, per stirpes, or, if none, [[END IF]]to those persons who would be entitled to inherit from me, and in the shares provided, under the Tennessee laws of intestate succession then in effect, as though I had died at that time domiciled in Tennessee.

If any person entitled to a distribution under Section M has a disability and, in the Trustee’s judgment, receives or may become eligible for means-tested public benefits, the Trustee may instead hold that person’s share in a separate trust for that person’s benefit on terms identical to this Article.

**O. Additional Trustee Powers and Administration.** In addition to the powers conferred elsewhere in this Will and by law, the Trustee may: (1) retain or acquire a residence, or an interest in a residence, for the Beneficiary’s use; (2) purchase an irrevocable prepaid funeral contract for the Beneficiary; (3) employ attorneys, benefits planners, care managers, accountants, and investment advisors, and pay their reasonable fees from this trust; and (4) rely on the advice of counsel concerning public benefits. The Trustee may consider any letter of intent or memorandum I leave concerning the Beneficiary’s care, but no such writing shall be binding on the Trustee. To the extent permitted by law, the Trustee shall be excused from filing accountings with any court, but shall keep accurate records and shall provide an annual account to the Beneficiary or to the Beneficiary’s guardian, conservator, or other legal representative.

**P. Construction.** This Article shall be governed by the laws of the State of Tennessee. If any provision of this Article conflicts with the intent expressed in Section B, Section B shall control, and any ambiguity shall be resolved in favor of preserving the Beneficiary’s eligibility for public benefits.
